# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard

> **Interactive Excel dashboard for analyzing Sales, Profitability,
> Quantity, Discounts, Regions, States, Segments, Products and Shipping
> Performance.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. 🎯 Project Overview

The **Superstore Sales Dashboard** is an Excel-based business
intelligence dashboard built using the Superstore sales dataset.

The workbook combines:

-   Raw sales data
-   Excel Tables
-   PivotTables
-   PivotCharts
-   Slicers
-   KPI cards
-   Key business insights

The main goal is to turn a large transaction-level dataset into a
**simple, interactive and presentation-ready dashboard** that helps
answer:

> **What is selling, where is it selling, how profitable is it, and
> which areas need attention?**

## 3. 📌 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard provides high-level KPI cards for:

### 💵 Total Sales

Shows the total sales generated from the selected data.

### 💰 Total Profit

Shows total profit generated.

### 📦 Total Quantity

Shows the number of units sold.

### 🏷️ Average Profit

Shows the configured average-profit metric used in the dashboard.

> **Important:** KPI values can change when slicers are used.

## 5. 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Sales by Category

Compares sales generated by each major category.

**Business question:**\
\> Which category generates the most revenue?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2. Profit by Region

Compares profitability across the four regions.

**Business question:**\
\> Which region contributes the most profit?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3. Sales by Segment

Shows sales distribution across:

-   Consumer
-   Corporate
-   Home Office

**Business question:**\
\> Which customer segment contributes the most sales?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 4. Sales & Profit by Ship Mode

Compares sales and profit for each shipping method.

**Business question:**\
\> Which shipping mode generates the highest sales and how profitable is
it?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 5. Sales & Profit by State

Compares state-level sales and profit.

**Business question:**\
\> Which states are strong revenue contributors and which states may be
hurting profitability?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 6. Sales by City

Shows sales performance across selected cities.

**Business question:**\
\> Which cities generate the highest sales?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 7. Discount vs Profit

Analyzes the relationship between discount levels and profit.

**Business question:**\
\> At which discount levels does profitability become weaker?

This is particularly useful for identifying whether heavy discounting is
associated with lower or negative profit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 8. Product by Sales and Profit

Compares product/sub-category performance using sales and profit.

**Business question:**\
\> Which products generate strong sales, and are those products also
profitable?

## 6. 🔍 Key Business Insights

The dashboard is designed to highlight insights such as:

### 🏆 Strong Category

**Technology** is the strongest category in the full source dataset by
both sales and profit.

### 📍 Strong Region

**West** is the strongest region by sales and also leads in profit in
the full source dataset.

### 📱 Top Sub-Category by Sales

**Phones** are the highest-sales sub-category in the full source
dataset.

### 📉 Loss-Making Area

**Tables** have negative total profit in the full source dataset and
therefore deserve attention.

### 🚚 Shipping Performance

**Standard Class** is the largest shipping mode by sales in the full
source dataset.

### 🎯 Discount Risk

Higher discount levels can be associated with weaker or negative
profitability, making the Discount vs Profit analysis useful for pricing
decisions.

## 10. 🔧 Refreshing the Dashboard

If the source data changes:

1.  Update the data table.
2.  Go to **Data → Refresh All**.
3.  Refresh the PivotTables/PivotCharts.
4.  Check the slicers.
5.  Verify that the KPI cards and charts have updated.

### Important

Keep the source column names consistent.

For example:

`Sales` should remain `Sales`.

`Profit` should remain `Profit`.

Changing field names can break PivotTables, PivotCharts or slicer
connections.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11. ⚠️ Important Data Note

The current source dataset contains:

**Ship Mode, Segment, Country, City, State, Postal Code, Region,
Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Quantity, Discount and Profit.**

It does **not contain an Order Date/Year field**.

Therefore, a proper:

-   Year slicer
-   Monthly sales trend
-   Year-over-year comparison
-   Monthly profit trend

cannot be created from the current source without adding a date field
such as **Order Date**.

This workbook transforms raw Superstore transaction data into an
interactive management dashboard.

**Revenue leaders → Profit leaders → Strong regions → Strong states →

The dashboard can therefore be used as a compact **sales and
